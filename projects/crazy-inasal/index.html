Our Story

Bacolod Tradition, Edmonton Proud

Crazy Inasal brings the legendary charcoal-grilled chicken of Bacolod City straight to St. Albert. Every piece of chicken is marinated for 24 hours in our signature blend of lemongrass, calamansi, and native annatto oil before hitting the charcoal grill.

We believe in simple ingredients, authentic technique, and the kind of food that makes you feel at home — no matter where home is.
